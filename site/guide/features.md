# Features

## Practice

The home screen. **Today** is a queue of questions weighted toward your weakest and least
recently practised areas. Start a drill (a few questions) or a full loop (several
interviewers in a row) from the setup sheet.

## Live session

- The interviewer speaks the question; you answer out loud. A mono timer runs, and at 3:00 it
  turns gold to tell you to wrap up.
- **Hands-free:** say "redo", "next" or "hint". On desktop: Space pause · R redo · N next ·
  H hint · Esc end.
- **Coaching** follows each answer: one strength, up to three fixes, and a verdict, read out
  loud and saved as a card.
- The interviewer can look things up while it talks: your stories, matching questions, how an
  area went before, and (with [hints](/guide/hints)) your prep files.
- A dropped connection shows a banner and keeps recording; the session resumes.

## History

Every session with its recording, transcript, a card per answer and a session report. Play
back any answer from its waveform.

## Progress

Readiness per area over time, a heatmap of verdicts, and the weakest answer to redo next.

## Library

Areas, stories, questions and files. Edit stories in STAR form with numbers, map them to
areas on the Story × Area grid, and preview or edit uploaded files.

## Offline and install

The app shell, library, History and Progress work offline (service worker). Live sessions need
a connection; Practice says so and disables Start. The demo works offline.

The installed app has two shortcuts (long-press the icon on Android, right-click on desktop):

| Link | Does |
|---|---|
| `…/#/start` | starts today's suggested drill |
| `…/#/last` | opens the latest session in History |

On iPhone, an iOS Shortcut ("Open URL" with either link) can go on the Action button or a widget.

## Backups

Settings → Library & data. A zip with everything, the library only, or sessions only, with an
optional passphrase (AES-GCM). Restore can **Replace** or **Merge**. The Gemini key is never in
a backup.
