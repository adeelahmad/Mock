# Game

A quick drill on which area a question tests, without a live session. Open the **Game** tab.

1. A round deals 10 tagged questions from your bank.
2. For each one, tap every area you think it belongs to, then **Check**.
3. Scoring per area you picked:
   - **+5** for the primary area (the question's first tag)
   - **+2** for a secondary area
   - **−2** for an area the question isn't tagged with
4. The reveal colours hits, wrong picks and missed areas. **Next** moves on.

Finished rounds are saved (the last 100). The start view shows your best score, games played,
recent games, and accuracy by area with the weakest first, so you know what to read up on.

Untagged questions are skipped. It's all buttons, so mouse, touch and keyboard work the same.
