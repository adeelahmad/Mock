Decisions
  • Easy as 1 2 3. Mock already knows what you should practise, so the default path is one tap or none: Start is the home screen (and a lock-screen widget); coaching starts when you stop talking; the next question follows by itself; first run is drop → say hello → start; imports accept confident tags; grid cells map on one tap; verdict chips deep-link to the answer. Choices are remembered, never re-asked.
  • Light-first, per your note. Every board has a theme tweak; dark reuses Aladdin’s terminal palette.
  • Two mode accents only: gold = interview, blue = coaching. The top edge, the voice visual and the primary button carry the mode.
  • Verdicts use all four Aladdin colours, each with a shape: ✓ Strong hire · ◆ Inclined · ● Not inclined · ✕ Strong no hire. Gray ○ = not practised.
  • DM Sans for everything; JetBrains Mono with tabular figures for timers, counts and key numbers.
  • Phone artboards are 390 × 844 in an iPhone bezel, main controls in the thumb zone. End sits top-right, deliberately out of it.
  • Buttons, chips, cards, tab bar and sidebar are defined once in Foundations and repeated verbatim across boards.
Assumptions
  • Sample content is invented: an SDM loop on 30 Oct (19 days out), session 14 is the current one, five interviewers with abstract monograms.
  • Stories: Payments outage, Legacy data migration, Platform rebuild, Vendor contract pushback, Hiring bar reset, Nordics escalation and four more; 13 of 16 LPs covered.
  • The live screen is hands-free: coaching starts when you stop talking, the next question follows after coaching; “redo”, “next” and “hint” work by voice. Three buttons remain: Redo, Pause, Next.
  • In a full loop the LP stays hidden until coaching; in One-LP mode it sits in the header.
  • Variations where you asked: voice visual ×3 (Foundations), live layout ×3 (Live board), practice home ×3 (Practice board).
